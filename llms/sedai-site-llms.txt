# Sedai

> Sedai is an autonomous cloud and AI optimization platform. It continuously analyzes application behavior and adjusts cloud configurations in real time to cut costs, improve performance, and increase availability — managing over $3 billion in cloud spend with zero production incidents.

## Product

- [Platform](https://sedai.io/platform): Autonomous optimization for cloud, Kubernetes, serverless, storage, and AI workloads.
- [Integrations](https://sedai.io/integrations): Supported clouds, Kubernetes distributions, monitoring, and notification tools.
- [Pricing](https://sedai.io/pricing): Volume-based pricing plans and how billing works

## Solutions

- [Cloud Cost Optimization](https://sedai.io/solution/cloud-cost-optimization): Reduce cloud spend autonomously.
- [FinOps](https://sedai.io/solution/finops): Automate FinOps practices end to end.
- [Cloud Reliability](https://sedai.io/solution/cloud-reliability): Improve availability and prevent incidents.
- [Application Performance](https://sedai.io/solution/application-performance): Meet latency and SLO targets at the lowest cost.
